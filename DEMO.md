# 3-Minute Hackathon Demonstration Guide

This guide walks judges and evaluators through demonstrating the complete end-to-end NEXUS workflow powered by **Google Gemma 4** in approximately three minutes.

---

## Pre-requisites & Setup (30 Seconds)

1. Ensure the development environment is running:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
2. Open your browser at **`http://localhost:5173`**.
3. Verify server health & active Gemma 4 model:
   - Visit: `http://localhost:3001/api/health`
   - Confirms: `{"status": "healthy", "model": "gemma-4-26b-a4b-it", "isOpenWeight": true}`

---

## 3-Minute Live Demonstration Flow

### Minute 1: Judge Demo Mode & Synthetic Personas
1. On the Landing Page, observe the **"Judges & Reviewers: Test with Pre-Loaded Profiles"** bar.
2. Click **Profile B: Priya (Freelancer, ₹62k/mo)** or use the top banner dropdown to switch personas:
   - **Profile A (Student - Aarav Patel):** ₹20k/mo tutoring income, limited savings, building first credit history.
   - **Profile B (Freelancer - Priya Sharma):** ₹62k/mo variable milestones + retainer, WeWork overhead, 100% on-time equipment EMI.
   - **Profile C (Small Business - Vikram Malhotra):** ₹1.4L/mo café turnover, commercial lease & supplies, commercial machine loan.
3. Arrive at the **Dashboard** and note:
   - **AI Transparency Banner:** Clearly explains Gemma 4 open-weight model + deterministic calculation guard.
   - **Financial Trust Score Card:** Deterministic breakdown across 4 pillars (Income Consistency, Savings Habits, Repayment History, Expense Management).

### Minute 2: Interactive AI Financial Health Copilot (Gemma 4 Chat)
1. On the Dashboard, scroll to the **NEXUS AI Financial Health Copilot** section.
2. Select a suggested chip or type a question:
   - *"Why is my financial health score at this level?"*
   - *"How can I improve my savings buffer as a freelancer?"*
3. Toggle the language button to **"हिंदी / Hinglish"** and ask:
   - *"Mera savings rate kaise badhaun?"*
4. Observe the live Gemma 4 response:
   - Formatted in natural, conversational Hinglish.
   - Strictly references the actual verified ledger numbers (e.g. ₹62,000 income, 31% savings rate).
   - Shows model attribution badge: `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it (Open-Weight)`.
5. Switch to the **30-Day Improvement Plan** tab:
   - Observe the 4-week roadmap generated by Gemma 4 (Week 1 Awareness, Week 2 Expense Management, Week 3 Stability, Week 4 Review).
   - Check off a task (e.g. *"Audit Top Category Spending"*) and watch the progress bar update in real-time.

### Minute 3: Explain My Financial Profile & Financial Trust Passport
1. Navigate to **Financial Passport** (`/passport`) in the top navigation.
2. In the **Explain My Financial Profile** section, click **"Explain My Profile"**:
   - Gemma 4 generates a structured breakdown: Executive Narrative, Verified Strengths, Potential Risk Flags, and Practical Recommendations.
   - Shows deterministic indicators (Total Income, Total Expenses, Savings Rate, DTI Ratio, Trust Score).
3. Scroll to the **Financial Trust Passport** document preview:
   - Click **"Share My Passport"** to generate a cryptographic, time-bound share link (`/passport/shared/:token`).
   - Open the share link in an incognito window: observe that private emails, full ledger entries, and passwords are fully redacted, showing only authorized trust indicators.

## Demonstration Highlights for Judges

- **Real Gemma 4 Inference:** Real open-weight inference executed through official `@google/genai` SDK.
- **Zero Hallucination Guard:** Deterministic arithmetic protects users from fabricated scores or fake interest rates.
- **Multilingual Accessibility:** Full support for both English and natural Hindi/Hinglish.
- **Instant Demo Accessibility:** 3 pre-built personas allow testing all features immediately without entering manual data.
